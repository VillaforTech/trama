# Community conduct

Trama welcomes people learning unfamiliar software and people helping them. Participants may have different languages, experience, and access needs. In issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces:

- Discuss ideas and observable behavior respectfully. Critique code and evidence with concrete examples.
- Ask for clarification before making assumptions about another person's knowledge or intent.
- Respect privacy. Do not post another person's private code, messages, credentials, or identifying information without permission.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual attention, personal attacks, and repeated disruption.
- Accept moderation decisions and requests to stop an interaction.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful material, close a discussion, restrict an interaction, or exclude a participant. The response should reflect the severity, impact, and repetition of the behavior. Good-faith disagreement about product or technical decisions is welcome.

For a public interaction that needs moderation, use GitHub's report control on the affected content or ask `@VillaforTech` to review it without repeating private information. GitHub's [abuse reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) provide a separate channel for harassment or platform abuse, including reports concerning a maintainer. Report technical vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

These expectations apply to project spaces and to public communications where someone explicitly represents Trama. Reports and moderation are handled on a best-effort basis; this project does not promise a confidential incident-response service.
